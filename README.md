# Susu & Neko's Grammar Garden · P.2 Grammar Quiz 1

An iPad-friendly revision site for a Primary 2 grammar quiz (3 November, Day 4).

- Item 1: Object pronouns (me, us, you, him, her, it, them) · Grammar Time Unit 10
- Item 2: Prepositions of place (on the first floor, at the bus stop, on the platform, in the cinema) · Workbook 2A Units 1–2, Grammar Time Unit 6
- Item 3: must / mustn't · Workbook 2A Unit 2, Grammar Time Unit 19
- Item 4: Spelling of words about places, rules, seasons and weather · P.L.E. 2A Units 1–2, 2B Unit 7

Includes learn pages, four mini games (Swap it!, Where is Kitty?, at · on · in baskets, Green or red?), topic quizzes, a 20-question practice quiz, a spelling bee, a mistake book, stickers and natural recorded English voice.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step is needed.
